Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about finding hostels and unlocking manager contact details.

What is HOSTFind?

A student-focused directory concept for discovering accommodation around UCC.

How much does it cost to unlock hostel manager details?

The contact-details unlock fee is GH₵47.00 and is separate from hostel rent.

Does the GH₵47 payment include hostel rent?

No. It is a platform contact-details unlock fee, separate from hostel rent and any deposit.

Can I contact the hostel manager after payment?

Manager details should only be displayed after the server verifies the payment successfully.

Can I get a refund?

See the Refund Policy. The final policy should be reviewed and published before accepting live payments.

How do I know if a hostel is available?

Availability can change. Contact the manager to confirm current room availability.

Are all hostels verified?

No. Do not assume listings are verified. The operator must create and maintain a real verification process.

Can hostel owners list their hostels?

Yes. Submit the listing enquiry form. A review process is required before listings are published.
