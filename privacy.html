Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HOSTFind intends to handle personal information. Review and update it to reflect your actual business practices before launch.

Information we may collect

The booking flow may request a student's name, email, phone number and selected hostel to initiate a payment. Collect only the information required to provide the service.

Use of information

In a production implementation, information should be used only to process the unlock request, verify payment, provide relevant contact details and handle support.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Paystack. HOSTFind must not collect or store card details itself. The Paystack secret key must remain on the server.

Retention and security

Define data-retention periods, access controls, deletion procedures and appropriate security measures before launch. The current starter project does not include a persistent database.
