Refund Policy

Draft policy only — define a fair, lawful policy before enabling live transactions.

Unlock fee

The GH₵35.00 platform fee unlocks access to hostel manager contact details and is separate from hostel rent. Refund eligibility is subject to the published refund policy.

Failed or duplicate transactions

If Paystack shows a debit but HOSTFind does not confirm payment, do not immediately pay again. Keep the receipt/reference and contact support so the transaction can be checked.

How to request help

Contact the operator with the transaction reference, date and email used for the payment. Do not send card PINs, passwords or secret credentials.

Before launch

Set clear refund eligibility, timelines and contact information, and ensure the policy complies with applicable consumer and payment rules.
